🎃 Arena Roulette

First-person duels in a haunted arena, with Last Oasis-style directional combat. Limbs are optional.

WASD move · Space jump · mouse looks
Move the mouse left, right or up to pick a side. Hold left click to draw back, steer, release to swing. Hold longer for a heavy.
Hold right click and steer toward the incoming blade to block (no hints: watch their weapon). Right click while drawing back to feint.
